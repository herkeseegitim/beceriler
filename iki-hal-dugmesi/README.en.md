# iki-hal-dugmesi (Two-state toggle)

A short vertical video format: a small pixel world lives inside an on/off toggle, and one click moves it from one state to the other (empty board to finished lesson, messy desk to tidy desk). The skill gives the format, the 24-second timing on a 120 BPM grid, scene rules (pixel scale, glass knob, cursor, title swap without a grey frame) and adaptation limits.

Included: `ornek/baslangic.html` (offline canvas starter with `render(t)`), `ornek/kaydet.py` (Playwright frame capture plus ffmpeg to H.264), `ornek/NASIL.md` (how to use, in Turkish).

Requirements: Python 3, ffmpeg, Playwright with Chromium (optional install, in a virtual environment).

Idea credit: a day/night theme toggle video by the X account Lattice_Node. No code or artwork was taken; do not copy the original scenery or characters.

Brand colours and product names (Herkese Eğitim, ÇizTahta, YazTahta) are examples only. Replace them with your own.
